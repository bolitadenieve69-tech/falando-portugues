# Private Beta Guide

Falando Portugues is currently a private MVP for validating the voice-learning experience before any commercial launch.

## Goal

The beta should answer one question:

Can real learners have a useful spoken Portuguese practice session with the AI tutor on their own phone?

This beta is not meant to validate App Store positioning, pricing, branding, or paid acquisition. It is meant to validate the core learning loop.

## Ideal Testers

Start with 5 to 15 trusted people.

Good testers:

- Have an iPhone or Android phone they use daily.
- Are willing to speak out loud for 5 to 10 minutes.
- Can explain what felt confusing, slow, useful, or strange.
- Include at least two learning levels, ideally beginner and intermediate.
- Include at least one person who is not technical.

Avoid starting with a large group. Voice apps fail in concrete, device-specific ways, and small batches make those failures easier to understand.

## What Testers Should Try

Ask each tester to complete one short session first.

1. Install or open the test build.
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. Choose their level.
4. Choose a topic.
5. Start a voice session.
6. Speak naturally for 5 minutes.
7. Try one intentional mistake and notice whether the tutor corrects it.
8. End the session.
9. Check whether the history entry appears.
10. Send feedback immediately after the session.

If the first session works, ask them to do a second session on a different day.

Use these questions in a Google Form, email, or message thread.

### Device

### First Impression

- Was it clear what to do first?
- Did the app feel trustworthy enough to speak into?
- Did any screen feel confusing?

### Voice Session

- Did the microphone work?
- Did the tutor hear you correctly?
- Did the tutor respond quickly enough?
- Did the tutor sound natural?
- Did the tutor speak European Portuguese?
- Did the tutor correct you in a useful way?
- Did the conversation feel too easy, too hard, or about right?

### Problems

- Did anything freeze, disconnect, or fail?
- Did you see an error message?
- Did you need to restart the app?
- Did the app behave differently after locking/unlocking the phone?

### Learning Value

- Would you use this again to practise speaking?
- What would make it more useful?
- What topic would you like to practise next?
- What is the one thing you would change first?

## Success Criteria

Treat the beta as promising if most testers report:

- They understood how to start a session.
- The microphone and tutor response worked without manual help.
- The tutor response latency felt acceptable.
- They would use it again.
- The corrections were understandable and not annoying.

Treat the beta as not ready if multiple testers report:

- They cannot start a session.
- The tutor often does not hear them.
- The tutor takes too long to respond.
- The voice sounds wrong for Portugal.
- The app fails on common devices.

## Suggested Beta Rounds

### Round 1: Technical Smoke Test

3 to 5 testers. Focus only on connection, microphone, audio, and crashes.

### Round 2: Learning Experience

5 to 10 testers. Focus on conversation quality, corrections, level matching, and history.

### Round 3: Multi-language Readiness

Use findings from Portuguese before building French, Italian, and English. Do not clone the app until the Portuguese loop is stable.

## Message to Send Testers

Hi! I am testing a private MVP of a voice-first language practice app.

The goal is simple: speak with an AI tutor in European Portuguese for a few minutes and tell me what feels useful, confusing, slow, or broken.

Please try one 5-minute session first. Use your normal phone and speak naturally. If something fails, take a screenshot or write down exactly what happened.

This is an early private test, not a finished public app. Honest feedback is more valuable than politeness.
